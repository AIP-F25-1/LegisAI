# Background Video

## Video Requirements

Place your law-related background video here with the filename: `law-background.mp4`

### Recommended Video Specifications:
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
- **Duration**: 10-30 seconds (will loop)
- **File Size**: Under 10MB (for web performance)
- **Content**: Law-related scenes such as:
  - Courtroom scenes
  - Legal documents/books
  - Gavel and scales of justice
  - Law library
  - Abstract legal concepts
  - Slow-motion legal imagery

### Video Sources (Free):
1. **Pexels**: https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/law/
   - Search: "law", "courtroom", "legal", "justice"
   - Filter: Free videos, HD quality

2. **Pixabay**: https://pixabay.com/videos/search/law/
   - Search: "law", "court", "legal"
   - Filter: Free for commercial use

3. **Coverr**: https://coverr.co/
   - Search: "law", "legal", "court"

### Video Processing:
To create slow-motion effect:
1. Use video editing software (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, etc.)
2. Slow down video to 50-70% speed
3. Add smooth transitions
4. Export as MP4 (H.264)

### Alternative:
If you don't have a video, the app will fall back to a gradient background.

---

**Note**: The video will automatically:
- Loop continuously
- Play muted (no sound)
- Scale slowly for depth effect
- Have overlay for text readability
